    AI model

    Course Consultant uses your own AI account. You pay your provider directly; we never charge for AI.

    You'll approve access on openrouter.ai and come straight back. You can set a spending limit there.

    Bring in your course

    Choose one. Course Consultant reads text and structure only. Before saving, it removes likely student information such as emails and names in class posts.

    Upload a Canvas export

    In Canvas: Settings → Export Course Content → Course. Even multi-gigabyte exports work: videos and other media stay on your computer and are only listed by name.

    Make a smaller export

    Choose Select specific content, then untick Files. The export is quicker to make and download.

    Also include these documents as text? (optional)

    Use the browser extension

    Send the live course straight from Canvas: no export or download. It reads course content only, after you click and review it.

    1. Install the Course Consultant extension for Chrome or Edge.
    2. Open your course in Canvas and click the extension.
    3. Review, then Send to Course Consultant.

    Connect to your school's Canvas

    Your institution has approved Course Consultant. Sign in to Canvas and choose a course; it reads course content only, and the connection lasts one hour.
