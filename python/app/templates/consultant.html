Use the browser extension

Send the live course straight from Canvas: no export or download. It reads course content only, after you click and review it.

  1. Install the Course Consultant extension for Chrome or Edge.
  2. Open your course in Canvas and click the extension.
  3. Review, then Send to Course Consultant.

    Share a read-only link

    Anyone with the link can read the plan and the course site preview (for example an instructional designer or your chair). They cannot change anything or see your consultation or AI settings. The link shows your latest work, lasts 90 days, and stops working as soon as you turn sharing off.

    Quality check (automatic, no AI)

    Checks the consultation, plan and documents for common weaknesses: advice not grounded in your course, statements nobody made, missing topics or AI dimensions, vague outcomes, accessibility errors and personal details. It cannot judge whether the advice is wise; you are the judge of that.
