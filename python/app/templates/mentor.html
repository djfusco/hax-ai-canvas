2. Configure AI before chatting

Choose an AI provider, save your key, then choose and save a model. Or explicitly choose Guided mode for a walkthrough without AI.

Guided mode makes no AI calls and does not invent analysis scores. With AI enabled, course excerpts and your design brief are sent to that provider. Keys are encrypted server-side, never stored in browser storage or chat.

Choose how to work. AI is not enabled until you save a provider key and model.

AI drafting and final review can make several provider requests to assess instructional items and the proposed draft, including separate judgments for chunks of longer retained excerpts. Your selected provider may charge for each request. Model-specific limits and reasoning controls are shown below; automatic output is capped at 16,384 tokens per request for this workflow. Actual token usage determines charges; incomplete responses are not automatically retried with larger budgets. Bounded excerpts are used; assessment coverage, context reductions and truncation are disclosed. Chunk judgments are not aggregate item scores. Guided mode makes no AI requests or assessment report.
