📋 How to get your Canvas Token
  1. Log into Canvas
  2. Click your profile picture (top left) → Account
  3. Click Settings
  4. Scroll to Approved Integrations
  5. Click + New Access Token
  6. Give it a name (e.g. "Course Builder") and click Generate Token
  7. Copy the token and paste it above
